Tsung-Han (Hank) Lin

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the NYU Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences. My research interests lie in vision-language models and multimodal agents, with a focus on aligning multimodal systems to perceive and reason about the real world.

Before NYU, I was a research assistant with the UCLA NLP Group, advised by Prof. Kai-Wei Chang, where I worked on video agents, and with National Taiwan University's MSLab, advised by Prof. Shou-De Lin, where I worked on vision-language models for chip physical design in collaboration with the NVIDIA Design Automation Research Group. I hold a B.S. in Mathematics of Computation from UCLA.
